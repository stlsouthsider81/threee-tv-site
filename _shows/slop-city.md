## About the show

**Slop City** is a weekly comedy and improv podcast out of St. Louis, hosted by
**Libbie Higgins** and **Tina Dybal**. There's no format, no script, and no
topic that's off the table — just two comedians riffing, telling stories, and
talking as openly about mental health as they do about whatever ridiculous
thing happened to them that week.

Libbie and Tina met through St. Louis's improv scene in 2015 and launched
Slop City on November 17, 2018. Nearly seven years and 300+ episodes later,
they're still recording every week out of producer Randy Cash's dining room —
no sponsors, no notes, entirely listener-supported.

> "We aren't trying to be anyone but ourselves."

New episodes drop every Tuesday. Catch up on the full archive and stream
Slop City right inside the TV3 app.
